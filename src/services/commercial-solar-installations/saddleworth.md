# Commercial Solar for Saddleworth Businesses

Saddleworth's shops, hospitality businesses, farms, and commercial properties can dramatically reduce electricity costs with solar. We're based in [Prestwich](/services/commercial-solar-installations/prestwich/), serving the whole [Oldham](/oldham/) borough with [MCS-certified](/accreditations/mcs-certified/) installations.

Commercial properties use electricity during the day when solar generation peaks. Typical payback is 3-4 years, then 20+ years of near-zero electricity costs. Farm buildings and rural commercial properties with large roof areas or spare land are particularly well-suited.

## Conservation area considerations

Some Saddleworth businesses operate from buildings in conservation areas. Commercial solar installations generally fall under permitted development, but we'll check the specifics for your property and handle any planning requirements.
