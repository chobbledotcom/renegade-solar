## Shaw's housing stock

Shaw is a traditional mill town with a mix of terraced housing and newer, larger properties. Average prices around £335k reflect the slightly more affluent character of the area — homeowners here are willing to invest in their properties.

**Traditional terraces**: 3-4kW systems with 8-10 panels. The older stone-built terraces have solid roof structures well-suited to solar.

**Larger/newer properties**: 4-6kW+ systems with room for more panels. These homes often have higher energy consumption and benefit most from solar savings.

A 4kW system in the Oldham area produces around 3,250 kWh per year. Shaw households can expect savings of £350-570 annually, with more possible through battery storage and smart tariffs.
