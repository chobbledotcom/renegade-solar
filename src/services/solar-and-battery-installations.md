# Solar and Battery Installations in Manchester

We design and install solar and battery systems for homes across Greater Manchester. The work involves the roof, the wiring, the consumer unit and — with a battery — careful sizing to match how the household uses electricity. That's why it matters that an electrician designs the system and oversees the install, rather than a salesperson with a script.

Ashley's the person you'll be dealing with from start to finish. He'll do the survey, design the system, oversee the install, and be the one you ring afterwards if there's a question.

## Design and Planning

When you get in touch, a qualified electrician comes out to survey the property and design the system — not a salesperson. We'll go through the different installation types — on-roof, in-roof, [ground-mounted](/ground-mounted-solar-installations-manchester/) or flat roof — and tailor the design to your property and your energy use. Every property is different, but the same attention to detail goes into a small domestic install as a much bigger job.

This matters because we've seen other installers recommend unsuitable or even dangerous setups — batteries in lofts, for example, which contradicts NAPIT safety guidelines. When salespeople are speccing jobs instead of electricians, corners get cut. With Renegade, the person doing the survey understands the technical requirements and isn't going to recommend something inappropriate just to make a sale.

## Installation

Installs are carried out with minimal disruption to your home or business. We work with local roofers and scaffolders on every project — they're tradespeople we've worked with for years.

## Commissioning

The system gets tested and commissioned to the relevant safety and regulatory standards, and you'll receive the full electrical certification.

## Aftercare

We're available for any questions or issues with the new installation. Two years' routine maintenance is included as standard.

All equipment comes with at least a 5-year warranty, and some panels carry warranties up to 30 years. We mainly use [DMEGC](/dmegc-solar-panel-installer-manchester/) and [Trina Vertex](/trina-vertex-solar-panel-installer-manchester/) panels — both have long warranties and tend to perform reliably on Manchester roofs.
