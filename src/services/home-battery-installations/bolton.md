A home battery installation gives Bolton households a way to buy electricity when it's cheap and use it when it isn't. You don't need [solar panels](/services/solar-and-battery-installations/) to make one pay: the battery charges overnight on an off-peak tariff and covers the expensive hours, which suits the way most households actually use power - a burst in the morning, a bigger one in the evening.

[Renegade Solar](/about/) is an [MCS-certified installer](/accreditations/mcs-certified/) based in North Manchester, and every Bolton battery job is surveyed, specified and commissioned by [Ashley Merritt](/about/), a qualified electrician with over 20 years of experience. No salespeople, no call centre - the person who works out what size battery you need is the person who installs it.

## What a battery does in a Bolton home

Off-peak electricity on tariffs like [Octopus Go](/octopus-go-battery-installer-manchester/) can cost about a third of the peak rate. The battery tops up overnight, then runs the house through the expensive hours. Add solar and it also stores what the roof makes at midday for the evening - useful on Bolton's terraced streets, where the roof was never going to hold a big array anyway.

We mainly fit [Solax](/solax-battery-installer-manchester/) and [AlphaESS](/alphaess-battery-installer-manchester/) systems, from 6kWh upwards, sized against your actual bills rather than a package price. Batteries go in garages, utility rooms or next to the driveway - never in lofts, which contradicts NAPIT safety guidance no matter how convenient it looks on the day. Domestic battery installations carry 0% VAT until 31 March 2027, whether or not solar is involved.

One honest limit: a battery keeps the circuits it's designed to keep running during a power cut, not the whole house by default. If backup matters to you, we design the essential-circuit side properly rather than promising the earth.

## Retrofit batteries for existing solar

Bolton has plenty of roofs already carrying panels fitted by other firms. A retrofit battery adds storage to an existing [solar installation](/bolton/solar-and-battery-installations/), and the integration - inverter compatibility, export limits, tariff setup - is electrician's work, which is what we do. It's also the fix for the classic problem: panels generating surplus at midday while the household is out.
