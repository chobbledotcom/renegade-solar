## What a battery does in a Bury home

On tariffs like [Octopus Go](/octopus-go-battery-installer-manchester/), off-peak electricity can cost about a third of the peak rate. The battery tops up overnight and runs the house through the expensive hours. Pair it with solar and it also stores the midday surplus for the evening - handy in Bury's Victorian terraces and stone properties, where the roof often has character but not a lot of spare space.

We mainly fit [Solax](/solax-battery-installer-manchester/) and [AlphaESS](/alphaess-battery-installer-manchester/) systems, from 6kWh upwards, sized against your actual bills rather than a package. Batteries live in garages, utility rooms or beside the driveway - never in lofts, which contradicts NAPIT safety guidance however tidy it looks. Domestic battery installations carry 0% VAT until 31 March 2027, with or without solar.

One honest limit: a battery keeps the circuits it's designed to keep running during a power cut, not the whole house by default. If backup matters to you, we design the essential-circuit side properly rather than overpromising.

## Retrofit batteries for existing solar

Plenty of Bury homes already have panels on the roof from another installer. A retrofit battery adds storage to an existing [solar installation](/bury/solar-and-battery-installations/) - inverter compatibility, export limits and tariff setup included, which is electrician's work rather than a plug-in gadget. It solves the familiar problem: panels making surplus at midday while nobody's home to use it.
