# Solar Panel Removal & Refit in Bury

We remove and refit solar arrays in Bury for re-roofs, roof repairs and building work. Ashley is an MCS-certified installer and a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870), and we work across the borough from Prestwich.

## Why Bury panels come off

Bury has older terraces closer to the centre, larger semis and detached homes out towards Tottington and Ramsbottom, and a fair amount of housing that has been extended or altered over the years. The older roofs are the ones most likely to need a re-cover or a repair, and an array on top has to come off before the roofers can get at the covering.

Out towards Ramsbottom and the edge of the borough, the roofs are bigger and the trees are closer, so shading and access both matter. A re-roof is also a chance to check whether the array is still in the best position, rather than putting it back exactly where it was because that is where the rails happened to be.

## What the Bury job involves

Bury roofs range from compact terrace slopes near the centre to larger roofs out towards Tottington and Ramsbottom. Ashley surveys the array, the condition of the covering, the mounting rails and the cable route, then agrees the scaffold and the roofing sequence. On the bigger roofs, the array may be split across more than one slope, which affects how the panels are labelled and stored.

The removal is done with the DC side isolated first, then the panels and rails come off. The roof is left protected for the roofing team. When the covering is finished, the array goes back on with the fixings checked and any tired flashings replaced. The strings are tested before the inverter is restarted.

## Working with your roofer

You can use your own roofing firm. We have worked with the same one for years and with others we had not met before, so the coordination is not dependent on a long-standing relationship. The sequence, scaffold and storage are agreed before the first panel comes off.

## Checking the panels

Panels that have been on a Bury roof for years are checked for cracks, delamination and corroded connectors before they go back on. The rails and fixings get the same look. If a panel is beyond use, we will say so and check whether a compatible replacement is available.
