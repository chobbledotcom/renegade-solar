# Solar Panel Removal & Refit in Altrincham

We remove and refit solar arrays in Altrincham for re-roofs, roof repairs and building work. Ashley is an MCS-certified installer and a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870), and we work across Trafford and the wider area from Prestwich.

## Why Altrincham panels come off

Altrincham has a lot of period and older housing alongside larger semis and detached homes, and a fair number of roofs that are being re-covered or repaired. The older properties often have slate roofs, chimneys and roof shapes that make the mounting and flashings more detailed than a simple new-build roof. When the covering is replaced, the array has to come off and go back on without compromising the roof underneath.

Conservation areas and period properties also need care with the visible details. A removal and refit that puts the panels back in the same place with the same fixings is usually straightforward, but if the work changes the appearance of the roof, it is worth checking whether consent is needed before anything is ordered. Our [solar panels in conservation areas](/solar-panels-conservation-areas-period-properties/) page covers that side of it.

## How the Altrincham job is handled

Altrincham's older roofs often have slates, chimneys and detailed junctions, so the survey looks closely at how the array is fixed and how the flashings are dressed. Ashley checks the panel layout, the rail fixings, the cable route and the isolators, then agrees the scaffold and the roofing sequence with you and the roofer.

The DC and AC sides are isolated and labelled before the panels come off. The roof is left protected, and the roofing team gets a clear roof to work on. When the covering is finished, the array goes back with new flashings where the old ones have failed, and the system is tested string by string before it is recommissioned.

## Working with your roofer

You can use your own roofing firm. We have worked with the same one for years and with others we had not met before. On a period roof, the useful conversation is about the details: how the fixings are sealed, how the flashings are dressed and how the panels sit against the roof covering.

## Reusing the array

The panels are checked before they go back on. On an older roof, the panels are often fine and the fixings or flashings are the parts that need renewing. Anything cracked, delaminated or failing is replaced rather than refitted, and you get told which parts are being reused before the job starts.
