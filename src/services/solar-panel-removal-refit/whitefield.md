# Solar Panel Removal & Refit in Whitefield

We remove and refit solar arrays in Whitefield for re-roofs, roof repairs and building work. Ashley is an MCS-certified installer and a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870), and we are based just over in Prestwich.

## Why Whitefield panels come off

Whitefield has terraces around the centre, post-war semis around Besses o' th' Barn and Hillock, and newer developments on the edges. The older terraces and semis are the ones most likely to need a re-roof or a repair, and an array on top has to come off before the roofers can work. A loft conversion or dormer is another common reason: the roof shape changes, and the array has to be planned around the new structure rather than left where it was.

On the newer estates the roofs are usually in better condition, but a flat roof replacement, a storm-damaged section or an extension can still mean the panels need to come off. The roof sets the order of work, and the solar fits around it.

## The Whitefield sequence

A Whitefield re-roof often means a loft conversion or a dormer is part of the same project, which changes the roof shape the array has to fit. Ashley surveys the existing layout, the mounting method and the cable route before the roofers start, then agrees the new layout with you rather than assuming the old one still works.

The panels and rails come off once the roof is ready to be opened up. The DC and AC supplies are isolated and labelled, the panels are stored clear of the work, and the roof is protected. On the refit, the array is set out to the new roof, the fixings and flashings are replaced where needed, and the system is tested and recommissioned.

## Working alongside your builder or roofer

Loft conversions and extensions often have a main contractor as well as a roofer. Ashley will work with whichever team is running the roof, agree the dates and make sure the solar side does not hold up the build. The scaffold is usually shared, which is one of the reasons to plan the removal and refit as part of the building work rather than after it.

## The panels themselves

The panels are checked before they go back on. If the array is being moved to a new roof layout, the string arrangement may change as well, so the testing has to cover the new arrangement rather than repeat the old one. Any panel that is damaged or failing is replaced rather than reused.
