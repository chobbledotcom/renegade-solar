# Solar Panel Removal & Refit in Middleton

We remove and refit solar arrays in Middleton for re-roofs, roof repairs and building work. Ashley is an MCS-certified installer and a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870), and we are based just down the road in Prestwich, so Middleton is regular work for us.

## Why Middleton panels come off

Middleton has a mix of brick terraces, post-war semis, newer estates and properties with extensions and mixed roof forms. The older terraces often have roofs that are being re-covered or repaired, and an array on top has to come off before the roofers can get at the covering. Chimneys, valleys and the junctions between a main roof and an extension are the usual places where a repair turns into a bigger job.

The post-war semis and newer estates are usually more straightforward, but a loft conversion, a dormer or a flat roof replacement can still mean the panels need to come off and go back on. The sequence is decided by the roof, not by the solar.

## How the Middleton job runs

Middleton roofs vary from small terrace slopes to wider post-war semis, so the first job is measuring what is actually up there. Ashley looks at the panel layout, the rail fixings, the condition of the covering around them and the route the DC cable takes to the inverter. If the roofers are replacing a chimney or a valley, that gets built into the sequence.

The array comes off in a planned order, with the panels labelled so they go back in the same string arrangement. The roof is then clear for the roofing team. On the refit, the rails are checked for corrosion, the flashings are renewed where needed, and the system is tested string by string before it is switched back on.

## Working with the roofing team

You can use your own roofer. We have worked with the same firm for years, and we have also worked with teams we had not met before. The conversation that matters is the one before the scaffold goes up: who needs the roof when, where the panels will be stored, and how the roof will be kept watertight overnight.

## Reusing the panels

Most arrays that come off a Middleton roof go back on. The panels are inspected for cracks, delamination and damaged connectors, and the mounting rails are checked at the same time. If a panel has failed, we will tell you whether a compatible replacement is available before the array goes back together.
