# Solar Panel Removal & Refit in Prestwich

We remove and refit solar arrays in Prestwich for re-roofs, roof repairs and building work. Ashley is an MCS-certified installer and a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870), and we are based here, so the survey and the roof coordination happen locally rather than from a call centre.

## Why Prestwich panels come off

Prestwich has a lot of Victorian and Edwardian terraces with slate or clay tile roofs, alongside semis and detached homes. Those roofs are now old enough that many are being re-covered, and a solar array on top has to come off before the roofers can work. The same applies to a roof repair around a chimney, a valley or a damaged section, or a dormer and extension that changes the roof shape.

On a Prestwich Victorian end terrace, we fitted an 11-panel array and a Solax battery after the roof had been replaced. That job was a new installation rather than a removal and refit, but the sequencing was the same: the roof work came first, the scaffolding was used for both trades, and the array went on once the covering was sound. If your panels are already up and the roof needs doing, the same order applies in reverse.

## What the job involves on a Prestwich roof

The survey starts on the roof, not in the office. Ashley checks the mounting rails, the condition of the slates or tiles around the fixings, the age of the panels, the isolators and the cable route back to the inverter. On a Victorian terrace the roof is often steeper and the access tighter than a modern semi, so the scaffold position is agreed with the roofer before anything is touched.

The array is isolated on both sides, labelled and taken down in sections. The panels are stacked clear of the roofers' working area, and the roof is left protected. When the new covering is on, the rails and panels go back with fresh flashings where the old ones have perished, and the strings are tested before the inverter is reconnected.

## Working with your Prestwich roofer

The roofing firm we use has been with us for years, but you do not have to use them. If you already have a roofer, Ashley will agree the sequence and the scaffold with them directly. The important thing is that the roof is ready before the panels go back on, and that nobody is working underneath a half-fitted array.

## What happens to the panels

The panels are checked as they come off. On an older Prestwich roof, the usual find is a few fixings or flashings that have reached the end of their life rather than a failed panel. Anything sound goes back on; anything cracked or delaminated is replaced. You get told which is which before the refit, not after.
