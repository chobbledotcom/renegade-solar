# Solar Panel Removal & Refit in Trafford

We remove and refit solar arrays in Trafford for re-roofs, roof repairs and building work. Ashley is an MCS-certified installer and a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870), and we work across the borough from Prestwich.

## Why Trafford panels come off

Trafford has larger semis and detached homes, plenty of hipped and pitched roofs, and a fair number of flat-roofed extensions and garages. When a main roof is re-covered or a flat roof is replaced, the array on top has to come off first. A hipped roof can also complicate the refit because the mounting rails and flashings have to follow the roof shape rather than a simple rectangle.

On the larger properties, the array may be spread across more than one roof section. That means the removal and refit have to be planned around the roofers' sequence across every section, and the cable route back to the inverter has to be protected while the roof is open.

## The Trafford job, step by step

On a larger Trafford house the array may cover a main roof, a hip and a flat extension, so the survey maps each section separately. Ashley checks the mounting system on each, the cable route back to the inverter, the isolators and the condition of the roof covering. The scaffold and the roofing programme are agreed around those sections rather than treating the roof as one surface.

The panels come off in labelled groups so the strings can be rebuilt correctly. The roof is left clear and protected. On the refit, each section is re-fixed with new flashings where needed, the cabling is reconnected and the whole array is tested before the inverter is switched back on.

## Working with your roofer

Whether you use our long-standing roofing firm or your own, the sequence is agreed before the scaffold goes up. On a multi-section roof, the roofers may need to work on one part while the panels are stored clear of another, so the plan matters more than it does on a simple slope.

## What happens to the panels

The panels are inspected as they come off. If they are sound, they go back on the same roof or a different section if the layout has changed. Any cracked or delaminated panel is replaced, and the mounting rails are checked for corrosion before reuse.
