## Common electrical issues in Bolton

The terraced stock around Farnworth and Great Lever frequently turns up consumer units that have been swapped for a modern board at some point but with the original wiring left in place behind it, which can give a false sense of security about the state of the electrical installation. We also see a fair number of installations where someone's extended the wiring themselves over the years - added a spur here, put an extra socket there - without the earthing or the bonding being updated to match. That's the kind of thing an EICR picks up, and it's why a visual check on its own isn't enough.

The newer estates and the larger properties out towards Bromley Cross tend to have more recent installations, but even those aren't always straightforward. Extensions, loft conversions and kitchen refits all add electrical work, and whether the original installation was designed to cope with what's been added since is what the EICR testing establishes. We frequently find properties where the visible parts of the installation - switches, sockets - look relatively modern but the wiring behind the walls and the earthing are still on the original installation from decades ago.

## For landlords

Landlords are legally required to have an EICR every five years and one for each new tenancy. Fines for non-compliance run up to £30,000, and the local authority can arrange remedial work and bill you for it. Existing tenants need the EICR report within 28 days; new tenants get it before they move in. If the EICR inspection turns up C1, C2 or FI issues, you've got 28 days to sort them. As a NAPIT-registered electrician, Ashley can carry out the EICR and any remedial installation work that comes out of it, which saves bringing in a separate contractor.

Bolton has a strong rental market, particularly around the town centre, and we carry out EICR inspections for landlords across the borough on a regular basis. ## What's included in the EICR

The EICR inspection covers a full visual check of wiring, sockets, switches and the consumer unit, then calibrated technical testing for continuity, insulation resistance, earth fault loop impedance and RCD operation.

The EICR certificate and report are issued the same day, written in plain English so you can understand what's been found and what it means.
