## Whitefield properties

The older terraces commonly have electrical systems that have been extended in stages - what went in for gas lighting and basic demands has had a fair bit of modern electrical load added since, and the consumer unit and earthing don't always reflect that.

The terraces around the centre also tend to have smaller consumer units with limited capacity for additional circuits, which becomes an issue when someone's added an electric shower, a kitchen full of appliances, or an extension on the back. The EICR inspection picks up whether the electrical installation can actually handle what's being asked of it.

## Common electrical issues in Whitefield

The Victorian and Edwardian terraces frequently turn up electrical installations where the wiring has been extended over the decades without the consumer unit or the earthing being upgraded to cope. Consumer units that don't meet current safety standards, inadequate circuits for modern demands, and earthing arrangements that need bringing up to date are all common findings on an EICR in Whitefield. We also see properties where someone's upgraded the consumer unit to a modern board but left the original wiring in place behind it, which can give a false sense that the whole installation is up to date.

The post-war semis vary a fair bit - some have been properly rewired with a modern installation, others still have additions from the 1970s and 80s that don't meet current standards. The EICR tells you which situation you're dealing with.

## For landlords

Landlords are legally required to have an EICR every five years and one for each new tenancy. Fines for non-compliance run up to £30,000, and the local authority can arrange remedial work and bill you for it. Existing tenants need the report within 28 days; new tenants get it before they move in. If the inspection turns up C1, C2 or FI issues, you've got 28 days to sort those. As a NAPIT-registered electrician based locally, Ashley can carry out the EICR and any remedial installation work that comes from it.
