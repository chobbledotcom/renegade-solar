# Consumer Unit Upgrades in Prestwich

We replace consumer units and fuse boards in Prestwich, and we are based here. Ashley is a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870) with over twenty years on the tools, and he surveys the property himself rather than sending a salesperson. The work is carried out by the in-house Renegade Solar team.

## Why Prestwich fuse boards get replaced

Prestwich has a lot of Victorian and Edwardian terraces, particularly around the village and out towards Heaton Park, alongside semis, detached homes and bungalows. The older terraces were built long before anyone expected a house to run an electric shower, a tumble dryer, a heat pump and an EV charger at the same time, and the consumer unit is often where that history shows.

We see boards that have been added to one circuit at a time, boards with rewireable fuses and no RCD protection, and boards with no spare ways left for the next job. We also see houses where a newer board has been fitted but the earthing and bonding behind it were never brought up to standard. A new lid does not fix the installation underneath.

## What the replacement involves

Ashley looks at the incoming supply, the earthing and bonding, the circuits already in the board and what you want to add. You get a written quote for the board, the protective devices, any earthing work and the testing before anything is ordered.

On the day, the supply is isolated, the circuits are labelled and the old board comes out. The new unit goes in with individual circuit protection, the earthing and bonding are brought up to standard, and surge protection is fitted where the regulations require it. The installation is then tested circuit by circuit and you get an Electrical Installation Certificate, a circuit chart and labels that match the board.
