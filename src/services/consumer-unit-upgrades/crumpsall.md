# Consumer Unit Upgrades in Crumpsall

We replace consumer units and fuse boards in Crumpsall. Ashley is a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870) with over twenty years on the tools, and the work is carried out by the in-house Renegade Solar team. We are based a few minutes up the road in Prestwich.

## Why Crumpsall fuse boards get replaced

Crumpsall has a mix of terraces and semis, with plenty of properties that have been extended, converted or added to over the years. The consumer unit is often the part that has not kept up. We see old boards with rewireable fuses, boards with no RCD protection, and boards with no spare ways left after an extension or a new kitchen circuit has been added.

We also see properties where a newer board has been fitted but the earthing and bonding were left as they were. That can look like an upgraded installation from the outside while the safety behind it is still on the old arrangement. The survey checks both, because a newer board on old earthing is not the same as an upgraded installation.

## The Crumpsall replacement

The M8 job above included a hard wire check as well as the board, which is the right way to do it. Ashley checks the incoming supply, the earthing and bonding, the circuits already in the board and what you want to add before quoting. The written quote covers the board, the protective devices, any earthing work and the testing.

On the day, the supply is isolated, the circuits are labelled and the old board comes out. The board goes in with protection on each circuit, the earthing and bonding are brought up to standard, and surge protection is added where the regulations require it. The circuits are tested before the certificate is issued, and any circuit that needs remedial work is quoted separately rather than tucked into the bill.
