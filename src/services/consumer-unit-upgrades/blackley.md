# Consumer Unit Upgrades in Blackley

We replace consumer units and fuse boards in Blackley. Ashley is a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870) with over twenty years on the tools, and the work is carried out by the in-house Renegade Solar team. We are based a few minutes away in Prestwich.

## Why Blackley fuse boards get replaced

Blackley has traditional terraces, former council properties and some larger detached homes. A lot of the housing stock has been through several rounds of alteration: a kitchen refit here, a back extension there, an electric shower added at some point, outdoor power run to a shed or garage. The consumer unit is where all of that history meets.

We see old boards with rewireable fuses and no RCD protection, boards with no spare ways left, and boards that have been extended beyond what the original installation was designed to carry. In former council houses, the original wiring may have been updated once and then added to over the following decades without the protection being brought up to date.

## Replacing the board

The survey looks at more than the box on the wall. Ashley checks the incoming supply, the earthing and bonding, the condition of the circuits and what you want to add. In older Blackley housing the earthing is often the part that needs the most attention, and it gets priced into the quote rather than discovered halfway through the job.

The supply is isolated and the circuits are labelled before the old board comes out. The new consumer unit goes in with individual circuit protection, the earthing and bonding are brought up to standard, and surge protection is fitted where the regulations require it. The installation is tested circuit by circuit and you get the Electrical Installation Certificate, circuit chart and labels.

## A second opinion can save a rewire

One of the more useful jobs we do is telling people they do not need the work they have been quoted for. If a board can be repaired safely, or the problem is one circuit rather than the whole installation, we will say so. A consumer unit replacement is not the only answer we can give you.

That matters in Blackley because the housing stock is old enough that a rewire quote can sound plausible even when the wiring itself is sound. The inspection is what separates the two.

## Not sure whether you need one?

If you have been quoted a full rewire, get a second opinion before you commit. An [EICR in Blackley](/blackley/electrical-safety-inspections-eicr/) at £150 plus VAT tests the installation and tells you whether the board is the problem or the wiring is. If the report says the board needs replacing, we quote for that separately and you can take the work elsewhere.
