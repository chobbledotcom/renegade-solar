# Consumer Unit Upgrades in Whitefield

We replace consumer units and fuse boards in Whitefield. Ashley is a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870) with over twenty years on the tools, and the work is carried out by the in-house Renegade Solar team. We are based just over in Prestwich, so Whitefield is one of our closest patches.

## Why Whitefield fuse boards get replaced

Whitefield has Victorian and Edwardian terraces around the centre and along some of the older roads, post-war semis around Besses o' th' Barn and Hillock, and newer developments on the edges. The older terraces commonly have smaller consumer units with limited capacity, which becomes a problem when an electric shower, a kitchen full of appliances or a back extension has been added.

We see boards with rewireable fuses and no RCD protection, boards with no spare ways, and boards where the visible sockets and switches look modern but the wiring and earthing behind them are still on the original installation. The post-war semis vary a fair bit too: some have been properly updated, others still have additions from the 1970s and 1980s that do not meet current standards.

## Fitting the new board

The first visit is a survey, not a sales call. Ashley checks the incoming supply, the earthing and bonding, the circuits already in the board and what the new equipment will need. The written quote covers the board, the protective devices and any earthing or bonding work before anything is ordered.

Fitting a board in a Whitefield terrace or semi often means working in a tight cupboard or under the stairs, so access and cable routes matter as much as the board itself. The supply is isolated, the circuits are labelled, the old unit comes out and the new one goes in with individual circuit protection. The earthing and bonding are brought up to standard, surge protection is fitted where required, and the circuits are tested before the certificate is issued.

## A board replacement is not always the whole answer

Plenty of Whitefield houses need a new board rather than a full rewire, and we will tell you which situation you are in. A board replacement can also turn up a circuit that needs remedial work once the testing starts. That gets quoted separately before anything is done, so the price does not quietly grow while the power is off.

If you are buying a house or letting a property, an [EICR in Whitefield](/whitefield/electrical-safety-inspections-eicr/) costs £150 plus VAT and gives you a report on the whole installation, including the consumer unit. If the report says the board needs replacing, we can quote for that separately.

## Solar, batteries and EV chargers

A lot of our consumer unit work in Whitefield comes out of the renewable side of the business. [Solar panels](/whitefield/solar-and-battery-installations/), a [home battery](/services/home-battery-installations/) and an [EV charger](/whitefield/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) all need circuits, protection and spare capacity, and the existing board is often where the constraint sits.

Sometimes the existing unit has room and the right protection, and nothing needs changing. Sometimes it needs a larger board, an extra way or an upgrade to the earthing before the new equipment can go in. The survey works that out before anything is ordered.
