## Why Middleton fuse boards get replaced

Middleton has a mix of post-war semis, more recent estates and older brick terraces closer to the town centre. The terraces are where the oldest boards tend to be. What started as a small installation for lighting and a few sockets has often had circuits added over the decades, and the consumer unit has not always been upgraded to match.

We see rewireable fuse boards with no RCD protection, boards with no spare ways left, and boards where an earlier DIY addition has left a circuit that nobody can identify. On the post-war semis the installation is usually newer, but an extension, a loft conversion or an electric shower can still push an older board past what it was designed to do.

Another had a new fuse board fitted alongside sockets, interior and outside lights, and received the certificates and guarantee for the work.

## What happens on the day

The survey comes first: Ashley checks the incoming supply, the earthing and bonding, the existing circuits and what the new board has to carry. The written quote sets out the board, the protective devices and any earthing work, so the scope is agreed before the power goes off.

On the day, the supply is isolated and the circuits are labelled before the old board is removed. The new unit is fitted with individual circuit protection, the earthing and bonding are brought up to standard, and surge protection goes in where the regulations require it. The installation is tested circuit by circuit, then you get the Electrical Installation Certificate, circuit chart and labels.
