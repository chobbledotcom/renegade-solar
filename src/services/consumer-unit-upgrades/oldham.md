# Consumer Unit Upgrades in Oldham

We replace consumer units and fuse boards across Oldham. Ashley is a NAPIT-registered electrician (66870) with over twenty years on the tools, and the work is carried out by the in-house Renegade Solar team. We work across Greater Manchester from Prestwich and cover Oldham regularly.

## Why Oldham fuse boards get replaced

Oldham has a lot of terraced housing and post-war estates, and many of the older properties have electrical installations that have been added to in stages. What started as a small board for lighting and a few sockets has often been extended for an electric shower, a kitchen refit or an extension, and the consumer unit has not always been upgraded to match.

We see rewireable fuse boards with no RCD protection, boards with no spare ways, and boards where the circuits have been altered without the protection being brought up to date. On the post-war estates the installation is usually newer, but an extension or a new kitchen can still leave the original board short of capacity.

## What the Oldham job involves

The survey checks the incoming supply, the earthing and bonding, the existing circuits and what the new board has to carry. On the OL10 job above, the board replacement came with a new feed to the cooker, which is a good example of why the quote is based on the circuits rather than a flat price for a box.

The supply is isolated and the circuits are labelled before the old unit comes out. The replacement board carries individual circuit protection, the earthing and bonding are upgraded to suit, and surge protection goes in where the regulations require it. The installation is tested circuit by circuit, then you get the Electrical Installation Certificate, circuit chart and labels.

It costs £150 plus VAT, tests the installation and tells you whether the consumer unit is the problem or whether the circuits behind it need work.
