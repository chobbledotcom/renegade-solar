EV charger installations in Shaw and Crompton from a NAPIT-registered electrician. Based in Prestwich, covering the whole [Oldham](/oldham/) borough. Ashley personally assesses and installs every charger.

## Tram commuters - the smart setup

Shaw and Crompton's Metrolink stop with park-and-ride is perfect for EV owners. Charge overnight at off-peak rates on a tariff like Octopus Go, drive to the tram, take the 32-minute ride to Manchester city centre, come home to a charged car. Your daily commute costs pennies.

Compare that to driving the whole way on petrol or using public rapid chargers at the other end, which work out more expensive than petrol. The tram-and-charge approach wins every time.

## Pair it with solar

Shaw's slightly larger properties often have room for a decent [solar system](/shaw/solar-and-battery-installations/). In summer, surplus solar easily covers the 4-5 kWh your daily drive to the tram stop uses. In winter, you charge overnight on a cheap tariff. Either way, you're spending a fraction of what you would on petrol.

## What gets checked first

Because a charger is a new circuit on your installation, the survey covers the things that decide whether it's straightforward: the supply capacity, the state of the consumer unit, the earthing and bonding, and the cable run to where the car actually parks. On some of Shaw's older terraces that last one matters more than anything on the spec sheet. Anything that needs sorting goes into the quote up front - the consumer unit upgrade is included in the quote if it's needed, not sprung on the day.

## Repairs and relocations

If you've already got a charger that's stopped working, Ashley can diagnose whether the fault is in the unit, its supply, the earthing or the software, and deal with the manufacturer where the box itself needs support or replacement. And if you're moving house within Shaw or further afield, we do [EV charger relocations](/ev-charger-relocation-manchester/) - off the old property, certified and refitted at the new one.
