EV charger installations in Lees from a NAPIT-registered electrician. Based in Prestwich, covering the whole [Oldham](/oldham/) borough. Ashley personally assesses your property and installs your charger.

## Lees to Manchester - the real costs

Whether you're driving into Manchester or heading to Oldham town centre, home charging beats every alternative. A year of petrol commuting costs several times what the same miles cost on a cheap overnight home-charging tariff. Public rapid chargers at peak rates are worse than petrol - they only make sense for occasional long trips.

If you've got [solar panels](/lees/solar-and-battery-installations/), summer commuting costs nothing - surplus solar covers a typical daily drive with room to spare.

## Properties heading uphill

Lees runs from the valley bottom up towards Springhead and Grasscroft. The lower terraces are typical Oldham housing - some with driveways, some without. For terraces without off-street parking, we run armoured cable to rear yards or install front-mounted chargers with proper cable management.

The larger properties higher up almost always have driveways or garages, making installation straightforward. Either way, it's a one-day job.

## What we check before we quote

A charger is a new circuit on your electrical installation, so it gets treated like one. Ashley checks the supply, the consumer unit, the earthing and bonding, and the cable route to where the car parks before quoting - which is why an EV charger installed by an electrician doesn't come with surprises halfway through the day. If the consumer unit needs upgrading first, that goes in the quote rather than appearing later as an extra.

The units themselves are weatherproof and smart, so charging happens automatically in the cheapest window on your tariff. Where you've got [solar panels and a battery](/lees/solar-and-battery-installations/), the charger can be set up to take the surplus generation in summer and charge from the grid overnight the rest of the year.

## Repairs and relocations

We also diagnose [EV charger faults](/services/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) - working out whether the problem is the unit, its supply, the earthing or the software - and we take chargers off one house and refit them at another when people move. If a unit is dead and out of support, we'll say so and quote for a replacement instead of charging you to keep looking at it.
