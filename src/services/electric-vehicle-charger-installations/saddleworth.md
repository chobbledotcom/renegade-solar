## Rural living, smart charging

Saddleworth's rural location means most households run at least one car — often two. An EV with home charging transforms your running costs. A typical commute to Manchester or Oldham costs around £675 a year in petrol. On an off-peak home charger, that's £90-135. With [solar panels](/services/solar-and-battery-installations/saddleworth/) generating surplus energy, your summer driving is essentially free.

For two-car households, the savings double. Combine a larger [solar system](/services/solar-and-battery-installations/saddleworth/) with battery storage and two EV chargers, and you're looking at thousands saved annually on fuel.
