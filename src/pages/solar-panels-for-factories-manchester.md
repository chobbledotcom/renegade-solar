## Why factories are well-suited to solar

The financial case for [commercial solar](/services/commercial-solar-installations/) is strong on a factory:

- **Production equipment runs through the day** - matched to peak solar generation
- **Three-phase supplies** can usually be tied into a properly sized system
- **Roof areas are typically large** and often uncluttered
- **Single ownership** of the building, which is required for the payback to work
- **Long operational outlook** - manufacturers don't tend to move

Because you're using nearly every kilowatt-hour your panels produce as it's generated, you don't need [battery storage](/services/home-battery-installations/) to make the numbers work the way a domestic property does. Most commercial installations reduce monthly costs by **70-100%, with payback typically within 3-4 years**, followed by 20+ years of low-cost energy.

Bolton's industrial heritage means many factories and manufacturing facilities with heavy machinery consumption still operate across the area, and Greater Manchester more broadly is full of owner-occupied production buildings with large roofs that are perfectly suited to commercial solar.

## Roof and ground options

Manufacturing buildings come in every shape, and Ashley designs for whatever's there:

- **Pitched roofs** on factory units work well, particularly larger commercial buildings with extensive roof space
- **[Flat roofs](/flat-roof-commercial-solar-manchester/)** give us flexibility to point panels south regardless of building orientation
- **[Ground-mounted systems](/ground-mounted-solar-installations-manchester/)** are a strong option if you have spare land, grass verges, or yard space - useful when you need more generation capacity than the roof alone can provide, or when you want to power [EV charging points](/solar-carports-workplace-ev-charging-manchester/) for staff vehicles

## What you need to qualify

The main requirement is that **you own the building**. You'll also need:

- Sufficient roof space or land
- High enough electricity consumption to give a strong return - the more you spend, the faster the payback
- A plan to keep the property long-term to maximise the financial benefits
