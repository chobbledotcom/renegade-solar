## Solar for factories and manufacturing

Factories and manufacturing facilities benefit significantly from solar because heavy machinery and production equipment mean high electricity consumption. The more you're spending now, the faster your payback period.

Bolton's industrial heritage means many factories and manufacturing facilities with heavy machinery consumption still operate across the area, and Greater Manchester more broadly is full of owner-occupied production buildings with large roofs that are perfectly suited to commercial solar. Most commercial installations reduce monthly costs by **70-100%, with payback typically within 3-4 years**, followed by 20+ years of low-cost energy.

## Why factories are well-suited to solar

The financial case for [commercial solar](/services/commercial-solar-installations/) is strong on a factory because:

- **Production equipment runs through the day** - matched to peak solar generation
- **Three-phase supplies** can usually be tied into a properly sized system
- **Roof areas are typically large** and often uncluttered
- **Single ownership** of the building, which is required for the payback to work
- **Long operational outlook** - manufacturers don't tend to move

Because you're using nearly every kilowatt-hour your panels produce as it's generated, you don't need [battery storage](/services/home-battery-installations/) to make the numbers work the way a domestic property does. That dramatically improves the return on investment.

## Roof and ground options

Manufacturing buildings come in every shape, and we design for whatever's there:

- **Pitched roofs** on factory units work well, particularly larger commercial buildings with extensive roof space
- **Flat roofs** give us flexibility to point panels south regardless of building orientation
- **Ground-mounted systems** are a strong option if you have spare land, grass verges, or yard space - useful when you need more generation capacity than the roof alone can provide, or when you want to power [EV charging points](/services/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) for staff vehicles

## What you need to qualify

The main requirement is that **you own the building**. You'll also need:

- Sufficient roof space or land
- High enough electricity consumption to give a strong return - the more you spend, the faster the payback
- A plan to keep the property long-term to maximise the financial benefits
