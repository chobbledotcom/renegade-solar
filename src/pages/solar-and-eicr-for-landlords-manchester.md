## EICR for rental properties - £150 plus VAT, same day certificates

The fixed price is £150 plus VAT for any domestic property - one-bed flat or five-bed HMO, the price is the same. You get the inspection and all certificates on the same day.

The legal position for landlords:

- An EICR is required every 5 years, plus one for each new tenant
- Existing tenants must have the report within 28 days of the inspection
- New tenants must have the report before they move in
- Any C1, C2 or FI issues must be fixed within 28 days
- HMOs are subject to the same 5-year cycle

Penalties for non-compliance are serious - fines up to £30,000, with local authorities able to arrange the remedial work and bill you for it. Tenancy deposit schemes can also get involved.

## What gets checked

A proper EICR isn't a quick visual. The visual inspection covers wiring, switches, sockets and the consumer unit - looking for damage, signs of overheating, and general wear. The electrical testing side is where the technical work happens: continuity testing, insulation resistance, earth fault loop impedance, and RCD testing to confirm the trip switches actually work when they're needed.

You get a detailed report and all certificates on the same day, explained in plain English. C1 means immediate danger; C2 is potentially dangerous; C3 is "could be better but not dangerous"; FI means we need to investigate further.

## Older rental stock - what to expect

A lot of rental properties in North Manchester are older. Victorian terraces, common in Prestwich and Whitefield, often have old wiring systems like cloth-covered cables, inadequate earthing (proper earthing wasn't standard when these houses were built), circuits designed for gas lighting and a few basic appliances rather than modern loads, and ancient fuse boxes with no RCD protection. Around 70% of electrical fires happen in buildings built before 1950, so a proper EICR matters more on older stock, not less.

New builds aren't automatically safe either - around one in five has electrical faults of some kind, which is why the [new-build EICR](/eicr-new-build-properties-manchester/) service exists.
