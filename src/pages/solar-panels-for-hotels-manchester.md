## Solar for hotels, guest houses and hospitality

Hotels and guest houses are running kitchens, heating, hot water, lighting and laundry from early morning to late evening, every day of the year. That's a lot of electricity going through the meter - and a strong case for [commercial solar](/services/commercial-solar-installations/).

Properties like Premier Inns are a good example of buildings that suit solar well: extensive roof space, daytime and evening occupancy, and a long-term operational outlook. The same logic applies to independent hotels, B&Bs, country pubs with rooms, and small chains that own their buildings.

## Why hospitality businesses benefit

The financial case for hospitality is similar to other commercial property types - because you're consuming electricity throughout the day when [solar generation](/services/solar-and-battery-installations/) is at its peak, you use nearly every kilowatt-hour your panels produce. Most commercial installations reduce monthly costs by **70-100%, with payback typically within 3-4 years**. After payback, you're looking at 20+ years of low-cost energy and a strong selling point for environmentally minded guests.

Hotels are a particularly good fit because:

- **Demand is constant** - kitchens, heating, hot water, and HVAC run year-round
- **Roofs are often large and uncluttered**, both flat and pitched
- **Owner-occupiers** can realise the full payback themselves
- **Sustainability credentials** matter increasingly to corporate bookings, weddings and event business

## Roof types we work with

Larger commercial buildings, including hotels, often have extensive roof space that's suitable for solar panel arrays. We design for whatever's there:

- **Flat roofs** let us mount panels pointing south regardless of building orientation - ideal flexibility for hotels with awkwardly oriented buildings
- **Pitched roofs** on larger commercial buildings work well too
- **Ground-mounted systems** can supplement rooftop installations or power [EV charging](/services/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) for guest car parks

EV charging is increasingly something guests look for. We've installed ground-mounted systems connected to outdoor fuse boards specifically to power free EV charging - in a guest-parking context that's a clear differentiator versus a hotel down the road that doesn't offer it.

## What you need to qualify

The main requirement is that **you own the building** - solar installations on leased properties don't work because you need long-term ownership to realise the payback. You'll also need:

- Enough roof space or land for a meaningful system
- High enough electricity consumption to give a strong return
- Plans to keep the property long-term
