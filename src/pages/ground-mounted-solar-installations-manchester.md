## Ground-mounted solar - when the roof isn't the answer

Most solar installations end up on a roof. But there are plenty of properties where ground-mounted panels are a better fit - or where they complement a rooftop array to give you more generation than the roof alone could provide.

We install ground-mounted systems regularly across Greater Manchester, both on domestic gardens and on commercial sites with available land.

## When ground-mount makes sense

Common reasons we end up recommending ground-mounted panels:

- **Garden space available** - and a south-facing pitch isn't possible from the roof
- **Listed buildings or conservation areas** - where roof installation isn't permitted or isn't aesthetically acceptable
- **Roof condition or age** - where the roof isn't suitable for panels without significant work
- **Shaded roofs** - trees, neighbouring buildings or the roof's orientation make it underperform
- **Want more generation than the roof can support** - ground-mount supplements rooftop panels
- **Commercial sites with spare land** - grass verges, yard space, set-back from the building

## Commercial ground-mount

Ground-mounted systems are a strong option on commercial sites with spare land, grass verges, or outdoor space. They work particularly well for:

- **Powering [EV charging points](/services/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) for staff vehicles** - we've installed ground-mounted systems connected to outdoor fuse boards specifically for this. Free EV charging for staff, no recurring electricity cost.
- **Supplementing rooftop installations** when you need more generation than the roof alone can provide
- **Off-grid sites** - agricultural buildings, mobile catering pitches, remote offices - where mains supply is uneconomic. See our [off-grid solar page](/off-grid-solar-installations-manchester/) for that side of things.

## Domestic ground-mount

For homeowners, the use case is usually one of three things:

1. **Roof isn't viable** - listed building, conservation issue, structural concern, shading
2. **More generation needed than the roof can support** - belt-and-braces approach with both roof and garden arrays
3. **Aesthetic preference** - panels you don't see from the front of the house

We design domestic ground-mount installs to suit the garden - frame heights, clearances, planting around the array - so the system fits the property rather than dominating it.

## The technical bits

Ground-mount frames are anchored either by ground screws or concrete footings depending on soil conditions and array size. We do all the cabling back to the consumer unit (or a separate fuse board for outdoor work) using armoured cable rated for outdoor installation.

We use quality [DMEGC](/dmegc-solar-panel-installer-manchester/) and [Trina Vertex](/trina-vertex-solar-panel-installer-manchester/) panels - both N-type modules that perform well in our northern climate and come with long warranties. Battery integration is straightforward; we typically pair with **Solax** or **AlphaESS** systems where battery storage is part of the design.

## Planning permission

Most domestic ground-mounted solar systems fall under permitted development, but the rules vary by property type, location and array size. If you're in a conservation area or close to a listed building, we'll talk you through what may be needed. We handle building control notifications and DNO (grid connection) applications as part of every install.
