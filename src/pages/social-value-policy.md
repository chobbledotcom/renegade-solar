# Social Value Policy

**Renegade Solar Ltd**

Social value matters to our team at Renegade Solar. We believe renewable energy should create positive outcomes for the environment, local communities, and the wider economy. Our social value approach focuses on reducing carbon emissions, supporting local employment, and contributing to sustainable communities across Greater Manchester.

## Environmental Impact

- Deliver renewable energy solutions that reduce carbon emissions
- Minimise waste and promote recycling on every project
- Encourage energy efficiency alongside our solar installations

## Local Communities

We are committed to giving back to the communities we serve in North Manchester and beyond:

- **Local suppliers and contractors** -- we prioritise working with local roofers, scaffolders, and tradespeople
- **Community energy education** -- we are currently working with Manchester University, designing a mobile unit that will travel around Greater Manchester educating the public on how solar energy works
- **Solar energy advice** -- we offer educational sessions on solar energy for homeowners and businesses
- **The Lighthouse Project** -- we support this local charity by donating new toys and clothes every Christmas
- **Blackley Lions FC** -- we proudly sponsor this local grassroots football team

## Employment & Skills

- Provide apprenticeships and training opportunities where possible
- Invest in employee development and skills progression

## Ethical Business Practices

- Maintain fair working conditions across all projects
- Ensure suppliers follow ethical and environmental standards
- Promote diversity and equal opportunities

## How We Deliver Social Value

- Sub-contract local roofers and scaffolders to support the local economy
- Source materials from responsible suppliers where possible
- Reduce company travel emissions by using remote consultations over WhatsApp and video calls
- Provide solar education resources to local homeowners and businesses
- Track environmental benefits from every installation

## Our Impact So Far

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Solar systems installed | 50+ |

## Policy Review

This Social Value Policy is reviewed annually by company management to ensure Renegade Solar continues to deliver positive environmental and social outcomes.
