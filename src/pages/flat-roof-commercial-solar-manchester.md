## A flat commercial roof is one of the best places to put solar in the UK

Flat roofs give us flexibility that pitched roofs don't - panels can be mounted pointing south regardless of how the building is oriented. That's why retail units, warehouses, factories and commercial properties with large flat roofs generate substantial energy throughout the day.

Many commercial buildings have large flat roof spaces going unused. We turn those into assets that produce savings for decades.

## Why flat roofs work brilliantly

The advantages compared to pitched roofs:

- **South-facing regardless of building orientation** - panels are mounted on tilted frames at the optimum angle, so a building that runs east-west still produces like one that runs north-south
- **Easier system design** - large unobstructed flat roofs can take bigger arrays
- **Easier access** for installation and any future maintenance
- **Less visual impact** - panels on a flat roof are typically not visible from ground level

For [commercial solar](/services/commercial-solar-installations/) on the right building, that combination is hard to beat.
