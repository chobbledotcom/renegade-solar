## What does a solar panel installation actually cost?

Between £6,000 and £12,000 covers most homes, depending on how many panels the roof takes and how many batteries you want installed. It depends on your particular home as well - which way the roof faces, what else is on it, how the cable run goes. The honest number comes from the survey, not a price list. Ashley designs the system and sends the quote for free; you only need to send your name, address and email. Domestic solar panel and battery installations also carry [0% VAT](/0-vat-solar-panels-batteries-manchester/) until 31 March 2027, applied on the invoice rather than claimed back, so the total on the quote is the total you pay. What drives the cost is covered on the [solar and battery installation](/services/solar-and-battery-installations/) page.

## How does it all work?

There are no moving parts. The panels generate renewable energy from daylight, cloudy days included, and the power comes in off the roof to the battery and inverter, which sit on the side of the house. The batteries charge up while you are out during the day, then run the house when you come home from work, college or school. Every day you go out, they fill up again, and you can charge them overnight from the grid at off-peak rates when you need to. For houses where the roof does not suit panels, the same idea works on its own - that is the [home battery installation](/services/home-battery-installations/) version.

## How much will you save?

Ashley's answer on camera is between 60 and 80% of the electricity bill written off, depending on how many panels and batteries you go for. Then he does what he would rather do anyway and shows his own bills. His house runs at about £40 a month with the standing charge in it, and that includes charging the electric van. The diesel van it replaced was costing about £250 a month in fuel, and the van now charges overnight when electricity is cheap.

Here are two of them - his actual Octopus statements, with the account number and address blanked out. The summer bill shows £52.23 of electricity for the month, standing charge included, plus £20.63 of gas. The statement after it carries a £94.15 credit for the electricity the roof sent back to the grid between July and October. Net the export off the import and add the gas, and you land close to the "about £40 a month" he mentions on camera.

One part of the bill the panels do not touch is the standing charge - the fixed daily amount every household pays before a single unit is used. Ashley's runs about £15 a month, which lands close to the national average: the [Ofgem price cap](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/your-energy-supply/your-energy-bill/energy-price-cap-unit-rates-and-standing-charges) puts the average electricity standing charge at 54.83p a day for 1 October to 31 December 2026, roughly £17 a month depending on region and supplier. That part of the bill stays whether the roof has panels on it or not.

That is one house with one usage pattern, not a promise about yours. The survey models the numbers for your actual roof, tariff and usage, and [are solar panels worth it in Manchester?](/are-solar-panels-worth-it-manchester/) covers the honest maths behind the percentages.

## Where does all the kit go?

The panels go on the roof. Everything else can go outside - in the video the batteries and inverter sit on the gable end of the house, keeping the indoors for living in. Batteries do not go in lofts any more; lithium batteries need sensible siting, and outside on a gable end or in a garage is where they do best. Inside is possible too if you would rather, and the survey settles where yours goes.

The [EV charger installation](/services/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) side is the same story: an electrician choosing the charger location, the supply, the cable route and the protection, not whoever turned up with a box.

## How long does an installation take?

Start on a Monday, usually finished by Friday, sometimes earlier, weather permitting. The roof work itself is commonly one to two days, and the scaffolding, electrical work and commissioning fill out the rest of the week. Every house is different, so the quote tells you what to expect for yours.

## What about warranty and certification?

Ten years on the batteries, ten years on the inverters, 30 years on the panels, and a five year workmanship warranty from us on everything we have installed. We are [MCS-certified](/accreditations/mcs-certified/) as well, number NAP-66870, which is what makes the installation eligible for Smart Export Guarantee payments from your supplier.
