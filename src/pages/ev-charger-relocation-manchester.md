## The same person at both ends of your move

Moving house with an EV charger is awkward. You've got a working setup at the old place, you don't want to leave it behind, you don't want to pay for a brand-new charger and install at the new place, and you don't want one electrician to disconnect it badly at the old house and a different electrician inheriting the mess at the new one. The straightforward answer is to have the same person remove it from the old house and reinstall it at the new one, which is what Ashley does:

An EV charger relocation covers both properties, with the old supply left safe and the charger tested against the electrical setup at the new address.

## Why same-installer-both-ends matters

There are a few practical advantages. The disconnection is documented properly, with electrical certification for the work at the old property - which is a clean record for the buyer of your old house and for any future sale. When Ashley arrives at the new place he isn't reverse-engineering somebody else's wiring, and if anything doesn't work afterwards there's no question of "well, that's not my work" because all of it is his. Brand and tariff continuity is preserved too: your existing OZEV-grant-approved charger, your [Octopus Go](/octopus-go-battery-installer-manchester/) tariff configuration, your [GivEnergy](/givenergy-installer-manchester/) integration - kept rather than re-established from scratch.

## Why this is worth doing

A 7kW wall charger is a meaningful piece of kit, and replacing it brand new at your next house means buying the unit again and paying for another full install - relocating is typically a fraction of that cost. Beyond the money, if you're already running an [Octopus Go](/octopus-go-battery-installer-manchester/) tariff, an OZEV-grant-approved charger, or a charger that's integrated with your home battery setup, you don't necessarily want to start over - taking your existing kit means keeping the configuration you already know works.

## What's involved

At the old property Ashley disconnects the charger safely and isolates the supply, removes the unit and any external cable runs, makes the consumer unit and any leftover wall fixings safe and tidy, and provides electrical certification for the work.

At the new property he surveys the electrical setup and parking arrangements, plans the cable route from the consumer unit to the chosen mounting point, installs with armoured cable rated for outdoor use where required, tests and commissions the unit, provides full NAPIT electrical certification, and configures the charger for your tariff and any home battery or solar setup at the new place.

If your new property's existing electrics aren't suitable for an EV charger - older consumer unit, capacity issues - he'll tell you up front and quote any necessary work separately.

## Solar and battery integration at the new house

He has experience with [GivEnergy](/givenergy-installer-manchester/) systems and can configure smart charging to match solar generation and time-of-use tariffs like Octopus Intelligent Go. He's also an [Octopus Energy Trusted Partner](/accreditations/octopus-trusted-partner/) and installs all of the Octopus chargers, so if you're switching brands as part of the move he can handle that too.

## What if the new property needs supply upgrades?

Ashley will check during the survey. Most modern domestic supplies have plenty of headroom for a 7kW charger, but if you've moved into an older property or one with an undersized supply he'll flag it. He can typically sort upgrades, or recommend you speak to your DNO if needed.
