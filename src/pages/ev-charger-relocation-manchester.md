Moving house with an EV charger is awkward. You've got a working setup at the old place. You don't want to leave it behind. You don't want to pay for a brand-new charger and install at the new place. It's not just convenient. It's better technically:

## Why this is worth doing

A 7kW wall charger is a meaningful piece of kit. Replacing it brand new at your next house means buying the unit again and paying for another full install - relocating is typically a fraction of that.

Beyond the cost: if you're already running an [Octopus Go](/octopus-go-battery-installer-manchester/) tariff, an OZEV-grant-approved charger, or a charger that's integrated with your home battery setup, you don't necessarily want to start over. Taking your existing charger means keeping the configuration you already know works.

## What's involved

**Removal at the old property:**

- Disconnect the charger safely and isolate the supply
- Remove the charger and any external cable runs
- Make safe and tidy the consumer unit and any leftover wall fixings
- Provide electrical certification for the work

**Reinstall at the new property:**

- Survey the new property's electrical setup and parking arrangements
- Plan the cable route from the consumer unit to the chosen mounting point
- Install with armoured cable rated for outdoor use where required
- Test, commission and provide full **NAPIT electrical certification**
- Configure the charger for your tariff and any home battery / solar setup at the new property

If your new property's existing electrics aren't suitable for an EV charger (older consumer unit, capacity issues), Ashley will tell you up front and quote any necessary work separately.

## What if the new property needs supply upgrades?

Ashley will check during the survey. Most modern domestic supplies have plenty of headroom for a 7kW charger, but if you've moved into an older property or one with an undersized supply, he'll flag it. He can typically sort upgrades or recommend you speak to your DNO if needed.
