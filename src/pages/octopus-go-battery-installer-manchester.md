## Save on bills with a battery and Octopus Go - no solar required

Most people think of home batteries as something that goes with solar panels. They don't have to. A home battery on its own, charging during cheap off-peak hours and powering your home through the day, can deliver significant savings even without a single solar panel on the roof.

We're an [Octopus Energy Trusted Partner](/accreditations/octopus-trusted-partner/) and we install [home batteries](/services/home-battery-installations/) set up to make the most of smart tariffs like **Octopus Go** - so your battery is charged on cheap rates and discharged when grid electricity is expensive.

## How Octopus Go works

Octopus Go gives you a low overnight rate for **5 hours every night** (the off-peak window) and a standard rate the rest of the day. Your battery automatically charges during the cheap window and powers your home outside it, so you're using cheap stored electricity instead of buying expensive peak-rate power.

To use it you need:

- An Octopus Energy account
- A compatible smart meter (SMETS2 or certain SMETS1 models)
- A home battery sized to your usage

(Octopus Go's window length and eligibility rules are taken from Octopus's own published page; the exact tariff rates change over time so we'll always confirm what's current as part of your consultation.)

## How much you can save

Off-peak electricity prices can be as little as **one third** the price of peak rates. A properly sized battery means most of your daily electricity comes from cheap overnight charging instead of expensive peak-rate power.

The exact savings depend on your usage pattern, your battery size and the tariff rates at the time, but the principle is simple: **the bigger the gap between off-peak and peak prices, the more a battery saves you**. With the way energy prices have moved over recent years, that gap has been substantial.
