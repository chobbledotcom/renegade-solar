## Solar on period and conservation-area properties

Solar on a period property in a conservation area takes a different kind of attention to detail than a modern semi. Slate roofs, original detailing, and planning sensitivities all need to be respected - but a well-designed installation can sit happily on a Victorian or Edwardian roof and generate substantial energy for decades.

We regularly work on **Victorian and Edwardian homes in conservation areas**, period properties in Bowdon, Hale and central [Altrincham](/services/solar-and-battery-installations/altrincham/), and converted apartments in historic buildings. We work with slate roofs on period homes and tile roofs on newer properties, maximising energy generation while complementing the architectural style.

## In-roof, on-roof, or ground-mounted

Different property types suit different installations. Ashley will go through the options during the survey:

- **On-roof** - the most common option, panels mounted on rails above the existing tiles or slates
- **In-roof (integrated)** - panels sit flush with the roofline rather than above it, often a better aesthetic match for period properties and conservation areas
- **Ground-mounted** - if the roof isn't suitable or the property has garden space, ground-mounted arrays can be a strong alternative
- **Flat roof** (on extensions or outbuildings) - panels can be tilted to face south regardless of how the roof itself sits

## Battery storage as a standalone option

For properties where roof installation isn't ideal - listed buildings, restrictive conservation policies, properties with significant shading, or where aesthetics rule out roof panels - **standalone battery systems** are a strong alternative. They charge during cheaper off-peak periods and power your home when electricity costs more, delivering significant savings even without solar.

We work with quality manufacturers including **Solax** and **AlphaESS**. Battery sizes range from 6kWh upwards depending on your home's energy usage. Read more on our [home battery installations](/services/home-battery-installations/) page.

## Solar performance in our climate

Don't let Manchester's rainy reputation fool you. Modern panels generate electricity from daylight, not just direct sunshine, and actually operate **more efficiently in our cooler temperatures**. Manchester rain helps keep the panels clean for peak efficiency. You'll be surprised how much electricity your system generates, even during the winter months.

We use quality [DMEGC](/dmegc-solar-panel-installer-manchester/) and Trina Vertex panels - both N-type modules that perform well in low light and come with long warranties.

## Battery and EV integration

Period homeowners often want everything to work together. Where appropriate we can integrate battery storage and [EV charging](/services/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) so your home runs as one system - panels feed the battery, battery powers the house in the evening, and any excess can charge the car overnight on a smart tariff.
