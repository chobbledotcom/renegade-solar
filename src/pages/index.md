## Solar grants and money savings

What a system saves depends on your usage, how much of it falls in the day, the roof, your tariff and the export rate, so we work it out for your house at the survey rather than quoting a percentage. There's more in [are solar panels worth it in Manchester](/are-solar-panels-worth-it-manchester/).

You don't need a grant to have solar panels fitted, but a few things on the money side are worth knowing about. A renewable energy system cuts your energy bills and carbon emissions by whatever you stop buying from the grid, and the less you buy from the grid, the less a price rise costs you.

Domestic solar panel and battery installations carry 0% VAT instead of the usual 20%. The relief runs until 31 March 2027 and applies automatically on the invoice, and standalone batteries have been included since February 2024. Our [0% VAT on solar panels and batteries](/0-vat-solar-panels-batteries-manchester/) page explains what qualifies.

The Smart Export Guarantee pays you for electricity you export to the grid, and eligibility depends on the installation being MCS-certified. The [solar and battery installation](/services/solar-and-battery-installations/) page covers the process and the paperwork, including the documentation your supplier needs to set up export payments.

A battery paired with an off-peak tariff charges when electricity is cheap and runs the house when it is not, with or without solar panels. Our [Octopus Go battery installer](/octopus-go-battery-installer-manchester/) page sets out how the pairing works.

For households on lower incomes in England, the Warm Homes: Local Grant is a means-tested scheme for properties rated EPC D to G, delivered through local authorities. Eligibility criteria change, so check the current rules on the [GOV.UK Warm Homes: Local Grant page](https://www.gov.uk/apply-warm-homes-local-grant).
