## Why we fit Trina Vertex panels

Trina Vertex panels are one of two ranges we fit regularly across Greater Manchester. We rate them alongside our [DMEGC Infinity RT](/dmegc-solar-panel-installer-manchester/) modules - both are N-type panels that come with long warranties and reliable performance in our northern climate.

The choice between the two often comes down to availability, pricing at the time, and what suits your specific roof layout. We'll discuss both during your survey and recommend what makes most sense for your property and budget.

## What makes the Vertex range different

Trina's Vertex S+ residential modules use **n-type i-TOPCon** cell technology - the newer cell type that's gradually replacing older P-type panels across the industry. The main practical differences:

- **Better performance in low light** - useful in our climate, where you're not relying on bright direct sun
- **Lower year-on-year degradation** - panels keep producing closer to their original output for longer
- **Higher peak efficiency** - more power from the same roof area

The Vertex S+ range delivers efficiency around **22.5-22.8%** depending on the variant, with output up to **450W** per panel. Trina's published specifications include a **25-year product warranty** and a **30-year power warranty**, with first-year degradation of around 1% and annual degradation around 0.4% after that.

(Trina's published warranty terms for the Vertex S+ range are the source for those figures - we'll confirm the exact warranty in writing as part of your quote.)

## Performance in Manchester weather

Solar panels in Manchester don't need blazing sunshine to work. They generate electricity from daylight, and Manchester gets plenty of that even when it's overcast. N-type modules like the Vertex range perform better than older P-type panels in cloudy conditions and lower light levels. The rain actually helps - it keeps the panels clean by washing off dust and grime that would otherwise reduce efficiency.

Honestly, you don't have to. **Both DMEGC and Trina manufacture excellent N-type modules that'll serve you well for decades.**

What we'll look at on your survey:

- **Roof layout and dimensions** - panel sizes vary slightly between manufacturers, so the optimum array layout for your roof might favour one over the other
- **Aesthetics** - black-framed dual-glass options if appearance matters
- **Pricing and availability at the time** - the market moves, and we want you on the panels that give you the best value when the job is being done
- **System integration** - matching panels to your chosen inverter and battery setup

Either way, you're getting Tier 1 N-type modules with long warranties.

## Battery storage with Trina panels

Trina panels work well with battery storage systems. We typically pair them with **Solax** or **AlphaESS** batteries depending on what suits your setup, with capacities from 6kWh upwards. The combination means you can store excess generation during the day and use it in the evening when everyone's home and electricity is expensive.

In summer you can run your house almost entirely on solar and stored power. In winter the panels still generate decent amounts and you can [top up the battery overnight on cheap tariffs](/services/home-battery-installations/).
