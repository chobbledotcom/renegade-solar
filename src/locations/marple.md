## Solar panels in Marple

Marple sits at the edge of the Peak fringes, and that shows in the housing - stone terraces near the centre and canalside, larger semis and detached houses climbing the hills, and newer estates filling the gaps. What that means for [solar panel installation in Marple](/marple/solar-and-battery-installations/) is variety: a compact array on a terrace roof is a different design to a larger system on a detached house above the valley, and both are different again from a property shaded by the woods that half the streets here back onto.

Tree cover is the thing we check hardest. It's a lovely part of the world precisely because it's green, and the same green that makes it pleasant to live in can take a bite out of generation if it sits between your roof and the afternoon sun. We measure what the shade actually does across the day and the year before recommending a layout - sometimes it means repositioning the array, sometimes splitting it across two faces.
