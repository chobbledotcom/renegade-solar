# Shaw

We cover Shaw and Crompton for solar, battery, EV charger and general electrical work. The town has a mix of older terraces from the mill days and newer, larger properties out towards the edges, and most of them can take solar in one configuration or another.

A fair few people in Shaw commute via the Metrolink park-and-ride, which makes home [EV charging](/shaw/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) worth thinking about alongside solar - charging the car off your own panels rather than paying for petrol or public chargers. We're based in Prestwich, about twenty minutes away, and Ashley does the survey himself and oversees the install.

## Solar panels in Shaw

The older terraces are stone-built with slate or heavy tile roofs and chimneys along the ridge, so a [solar panel installation in Shaw](/shaw/solar-and-battery-installations/) is usually a compact array placed on the cleanest section, sized to what the household actually uses. The newer properties towards the edges have larger, simpler roofs that take bigger systems, and paired with [battery storage](/services/home-battery-installations/) most of the generation gets used rather than exported for pennies.

The stone construction that makes these houses solid also makes the wiring routes less predictable than in a modern build, which matters when you're running cables from the roof to the consumer unit. It's exactly the kind of thing Ashley looks at on the survey, because an electrician designing the system catches it before install day rather than during.

## Electrics in older Shaw properties

If the house still has older wiring or a dated consumer unit, it's worth knowing before you add solar, a battery or a charger to it. An [EICR inspection in Shaw](/shaw/electrical-safety-inspections-eicr/) costs £150 plus VAT with same day certificates, and it gives you a clear picture of what the installation can take. We do the upgrades too where they're needed.
