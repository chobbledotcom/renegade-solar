## Solar panels in Blackley

The traditional terraces round here have compact roofs cut up by chimneys and ridges, so a six or eight panel array on the best section often does more than trying to cover every face. It's still a proper solar panel installation - it's just sized to the roof that's actually there. The semis and former council properties usually have two roof faces to choose from, which makes orientation and shading the deciding factors rather than space.

Trees are the other thing we check properly. Blackley sits next to Heaton Park, and several streets round here have mature gardens, so we look at where the shade actually falls across the day rather than guessing from the map. If the main roof genuinely won't work, a flat garage or extension roof is often a better bet, and we design [flat roof solar](/properties/flat-roofs/) with weighted mounting rather than drilling through the membrane.
