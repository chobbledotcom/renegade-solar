# Royton

We cover Royton for solar, battery, EV charger and general electrical work. It's an area where we've seen more people thinking about solar as part of upgrading the house - either to take a chunk off the bills, or because they're planning to stay put for a long while and the numbers stack up over time.

Royton's housing is mainly terraces and semis, most of which suit solar in one configuration or another. We're based nearby in Prestwich, and Ashley does every survey himself and oversees the install, including the grid connection paperwork that comes with it.

## Solar panels in Royton

The mill-town terraces here are solid stone houses, and their roofs have the usual features to work round - chimneys along the ridge, slate or concrete tile, and clean sections either side of the stack. A [solar panel installation in Royton](/royton/solar-and-battery-installations/) is usually about placing the array well rather than going big: a right-sized system on the honest roof face beats a larger one half in shade. The semis and the newer builds towards the edges have more space, which is where solar with [battery storage](/services/home-battery-installations/) starts to cover most of a household's day.

The grid connection paperwork Ashley handles as standard is the part people don't tend to think about - small domestic systems are notified to the network operator after connection, and larger ones need approval first. Either way it's dealt with as part of the install, not left with you to chase.
