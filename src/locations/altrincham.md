# Altrincham

We cover Altrincham for solar, battery, EV charger and general electrical work. The area has a strong mix of older period properties through the centre, larger detached homes out towards [Hale](/hale/) and Bowdon, and newer developments around the edges, and we've worked across most of it.

Ashley does every survey himself. As an MCS-certified electrician, he'll talk through what'll actually work on your roof, what the realistic returns look like for your usage, and what isn't worth doing. We're based in Prestwich, around half an hour across the M60.

## Solar panels in Altrincham

The period properties nearer the town centre tend to have the interesting roofs: slate, chimneys, dormers and bays that split each elevation into smaller sections. The panels have to be laid out around what's actually there rather than dropped on in a rectangle, and the smaller the usable spans, the more the design matters. Our [solar panel installations](/altrincham/solar-and-battery-installations/) in the area are all designed on a proper roof survey, not satellite images, because on roofs like these the two often don't match.

The larger detached houses towards Hale and Bowdon usually have the opposite situation - plenty of roof, higher electricity use, and often an EV or two on the drive. That's where a bigger array with [battery storage](/services/home-battery-installations/) and a [home EV charger](/altrincham/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) working together makes sense, charging the car off your own generation instead of the grid.

Some streets around the centre fall within conservation areas, which can affect what's possible on street-facing elevations. It's worth checking early rather than after you've settled on a layout - we cover the practicalities on our [conservation areas and period properties](/solar-panels-conservation-areas-period-properties/) page.

## Batteries and off-peak charging

You don't need to generate electricity to save on it. A home battery charged overnight on an off-peak tariff, then run down during the day, cuts the unit price of everything it covers. It works with solar, and it works without - see our [Octopus Go battery setup](/octopus-go-battery-installer-manchester/) page for how the tariff side fits together.
