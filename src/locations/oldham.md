## Solar panels in Oldham

The stone terraces on Oldham's hillsides have slate roofs, thick walls and chimneys where you'd want the panels, so the design work is about using the clean roof properly rather than covering everything. Access on the steeper streets is part of the planning too - scaffolding and delivery all get thought about before install day, not during it. Further out, the semis and detached houses towards Royton and Saddleworth take bigger [solar panel installations](/oldham/solar-and-battery-installations/), and with a battery most households can use the bulk of what they generate.

The hills also mean the weather does its bit - panels run on daylight rather than needing blue skies, and they hold efficiency well in cooler air. What actually decides the output on any Oldham roof is orientation and shade, which is what the survey measures.
