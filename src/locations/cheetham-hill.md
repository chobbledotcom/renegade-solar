## Solar panels in Cheetham Hill

The terraced streets here are compact two and three bedroom houses with chimney-heavy roofs, so a solar panel installation in Cheetham Hill is usually about placing six to ten panels well rather than fitting as many as possible. Done right, that sized system still covers a serious share of a typical household's electricity. The newer developments have more room to work with, and the commercial buildings along the main roads out of the city are a different job again - see our [commercial solar](/services/commercial-solar-installations/) page if you've got business premises.

Where the main roof faces the wrong way or is too cut up, the flat extension or garage roof is often the better mounting option. We do [flat roof solar](/properties/flat-roofs/) with weighted mounting systems, which keeps the roof membrane intact and lets the panels sit at the right angle.

## Batteries without solar

Not every property here can take panels, and some are flats where the roof isn't yours to use. A [home battery](/services/home-battery-installations/) still works on its own - charged overnight on a cheap tariff, run down through the day - and it's one of the options we talk through when the roof isn't going to happen. For flats, the freeholder and lease questions come first, and it's better to have that conversation before the survey.
