# Renegade Solar in Bolton

We cover Bolton for solar, battery, EV charger and general electrical work, and we do a fair bit of commercial work in the area as well. We're based about twenty minutes down the road in Prestwich, so getting out for a survey isn't an issue.

Bolton has a wide spread of property types - Victorian terraces and stone-built houses through the older parts of town, newer estates on the edges, and a decent amount of industrial and commercial property along the main routes. We've worked across most of it. Ashley handles the surveys and oversees every install, residential or commercial.

## Solar panels in Bolton

On the older terraces, the roofs are usually slate with chimneys and shared party walls, so the design work is about finding the cleanest section and sizing the system to it. The post-war semis and newer estates out towards the edges generally have bigger, simpler roofs that take larger arrays, and with a battery in the mix most households can use most of what they generate rather than exporting it cheap.

Every [solar panel installation in Bolton](/bolton/solar-and-battery-installations/) we do starts with a survey by Ashley, an MCS-certified electrician - so the electrical side, the consumer unit and the cabling route get looked at properly before anything's agreed. If you're renting out properties instead, we also do [EICR inspections in Bolton](/bolton/electrical-safety-inspections-eicr/) with same day certificates.

## Commercial solar in Bolton

Bolton's industrial and trading estates are mostly big flat roofs, which suit [flat roof commercial solar](/flat-roof-commercial-solar-manchester/) well - weighted mounting rails, no penetration of the roof, and layouts that keep access walkways clear. Daytime-heavy electricity use is what makes commercial solar pay, so workshops, units and distribution sites around the borough are worth a proper look. See our [Bolton commercial solar](/commercial-solar-installations-bolton/) page for how we approach bigger systems, or the main [commercial solar installation](/services/commercial-solar-installations/) service page for the detail on design, DNO applications and payback modelling.
