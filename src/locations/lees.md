# Lees

We cover Lees for solar, battery, EV charger and general electrical work. Sitting between central [Oldham](/oldham/) and [Saddleworth](/saddleworth/), Lees has a mix of terraced and semi-detached homes, most of which take solar in one configuration or another.

We're based over in Prestwich and we cover the whole Oldham borough. Ashley does every survey himself and is on site for the install, so when you ask whether something will work on your property, the answer comes from the MCS-certified electrician who'll be doing the job.

## Solar panels in Lees

The terraces here are the older Oldham sort - stone construction, slate roofs, chimneys in the middle of the ridge - so a [solar panel installation in Lees](/lees/solar-and-battery-installations/) is usually a compact, well-placed array on the best face rather than a full-roof spread. The semis towards the edges have more to work with, and being this side of the Pennines the weather does its bit too: panels run on daylight, not direct sunshine, and they hold their efficiency well in the cooler months.

Because Lees sits on the climb towards Saddleworth, the ground rises and the aspect of the streets varies more than you'd think a couple of miles can account for. Two houses on the same road can have quite different solar potential depending on which way the roof faces along the valley, which is exactly what a proper survey settles.
