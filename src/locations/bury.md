# Bury

We cover Bury for solar, battery, EV charger and general electrical work. Bury has a wide spread of property types - older terraces closer to the centre, larger semis and detached homes out towards Tottington and Ramsbottom - and we've worked across most of it at one point or another.

Ashley does every survey himself. As an MCS-certified electrician, he'll give you a straight answer on whether solar makes sense for your property and what the right setup would actually look like. We're based in Prestwich, around fifteen minutes down the road.

## Solar panels in Bury

Closer into town, the terraces have compact roofs with chimneys and ridge lines doing most of the talking. A well-placed array on the best section beats a spread-out one that fights the obstructions, and because Ashley surveys every roof in person, the design matches what's actually there rather than what a satellite photo reckons. Out towards Tottington and Ramsbottom the roofs get bigger and the gardens get leafier, so shading checks matter more and the array sizes go up.

## Electrics first on older houses

Plenty of Bury's housing stock has been rewired in stages over decades, and the state of the consumer unit, earthing and wiring affects what can hang off it - solar, [battery storage](/services/home-battery-installations/) and an [EV charger](/bury/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) all depend on a supply that's up to it. An [EICR in Bury](/bury/electrical-safety-inspections-eicr/) costs £150 plus VAT with same day certificates, and it tells you where you stand before you spend money on top of it.
