## Solar panels in Radcliffe

The terraces closer in follow the familiar pattern - workable roofs with chimneys to design around - while the newer estates out towards the edges have larger, simpler roof faces that take bigger arrays. Between the two, most [solar panel installations in Radcliffe](/radcliffe/solar-and-battery-installations/) come down to orientation and honest sizing: enough panels to cover the household's usage, a [battery](/services/home-battery-installations/) to hold what isn't used straight away, and an inverter position that keeps cable runs short.

The town's position in the Irwell valley means some streets sit with taller ground and trees to one side, so we look at shading across the day rather than assuming a south-ish roof is automatically the right one.
