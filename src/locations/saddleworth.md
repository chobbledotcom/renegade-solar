Renegade Solar provides solar panel installations and electrical services across Saddleworth's villages - Uppermill, Greenfield, Delph, Dobcross, Diggle, and Denshaw. With larger detached and semi-detached homes, higher energy consumption, and more roof space, Saddleworth properties are excellent candidates for solar.

## Solar panels in Saddleworth

A [solar panel installation in Saddleworth](/saddleworth/solar-and-battery-installations/) is rarely a standard job, which is what makes the area interesting to work in. The stone slate roofs on the older cottages and terraces need proper care with fixings and weathering - slates aren't the same to work on as concrete tile, and the array has to be fixed so the roof stays watertight for the decades the panels will be up there. The exposed positions on the hills mean wind loading gets taken seriously in the mounting design too.

Orientation round here is decided by the valleys as much as the compass. Houses set along a valley side can have fine roofs facing unexpected directions, so the survey measures what each face is actually worth. On the larger detached properties there's usually room to combine faces and still size the system to the household's use, with a [battery](/services/home-battery-installations/) holding the spare for the evening.

Several villages sit within conservation areas, which shapes where panels can go on the visible elevations. It usually means designing round the street-facing roof rather than ruling the property out - we cover the considerations on our [conservation areas and period properties](/solar-panels-conservation-areas-period-properties/) page.
