# Chadderton

We cover Chadderton for solar, battery, EV charger and general electrical work. We already work over in [Failsworth](/failsworth/solar-and-battery-installations/) just down the road, and Chadderton sits along the M60, so it's an easy run from our place in Prestwich.

The housing in Chadderton is mainly terraces and family semis, and most of these roofs take solar without any complications. Ashley does every survey himself and is on site for the install, so the advice you get is from the electrician doing the work.

## Solar panels in Chadderton

The terraces this side of Oldham are solid brick-built houses with slate or tile roofs, and the design challenge is usually the chimney - deciding which side of the stack gets the panels and keeping the array in the clean section of roof. The semis give you two faces to choose from, so orientation and anything casting shade decide where the panels go. Our [solar panel installations in Chadderton](/chadderton/solar-and-battery-installations/) are designed around a proper look at the roof rather than a desktop guess, because the difference between a layout that works and one that doesn't is usually a metre either side of a chimney.

Newer developments along the M60 corridor tend to have bigger, simpler roofs and higher energy use, which is where solar plus [battery storage](/services/home-battery-installations/) starts paying for itself - generating through the day, storing the spare, and running the evening off it.
