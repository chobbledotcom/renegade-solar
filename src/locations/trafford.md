# Trafford

We work across the Trafford area, covering solar, battery, EV charger and general electrical work for homes and businesses. Trafford's housing varies a lot, from Victorian terraces and bay-fronted semis through to the executive new-builds out towards [Hale](/hale/) and [Hale Barns](/hale-barns/), so the right approach for solar varies depending on which kind of property you're in.

Ashley does every survey himself and oversees every install. As an MCS-certified electrician, he'll give you a straight answer on whether solar makes financial sense for your property and what setup would work best.

## Solar panels in Trafford

The bay-fronted semis that define much of Trafford's housing have two roof faces split by the bay, and the question is which face earns its keep - orientation and the shade from next door's trees decide it. The terraces nearer the older centres need compact arrays placed round the chimneys, while the larger homes towards the Altrincham end of the borough take bigger [solar panel installations](/trafford/solar-and-battery-installations/) sized to the bills. With a [home battery](/services/home-battery-installations/) in the system, the house runs on its own generation through the evening instead of buying peak-rate electricity.

Because Trafford spans everything from Stretford terraces to executive developments, we don't quote from postcodes - the survey measures the actual roof, the actual shade and the actual usage, then the numbers follow from that.

## EV chargers and electrical work

A [home EV charger](/trafford/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/) is a common add-on here, and with the miles people travel into the city and out to the motorways, overnight charging on an off-peak tariff pays for itself quickly. The charger gets installed after a proper check of the supply, earthing and consumer unit, which is standard for us because it's an electrical installation first and a gadget second.
