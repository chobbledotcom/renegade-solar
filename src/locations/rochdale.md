# Solar and Electrical Services in Rochdale and Heywood

We cover Rochdale, Heywood and the surrounding borough for solar panels, battery storage, EV chargers and general electrical work. ## Solar panels in Rochdale

Rochdale's housing runs from terraced streets around the town centre, through the semis and detached houses of the suburbs, to stone-built properties as the ground rises towards Littleborough and the Pennine edge. Each type wants something different from a [solar panel installation](/services/solar-and-battery-installations/). The terraces have compact roofs with chimneys to design around, so the job is placing the panels well rather than covering everything. The stone slate roofs on the older properties towards the hills need proper care with fixings and weathering, and the exposed positions mean wind loading gets taken seriously in the mounting design. The semis and the newer estates have larger, simpler roofs that take bigger arrays, usually with a battery in the system so the evening runs on the afternoon's generation.

Because the borough climbs into the hills, aspect varies a lot from street to street. Two houses a few hundred yards apart can have quite different solar potential depending on how they sit on the slope, which is exactly what a measured survey settles.

## Battery storage and EV chargers in Rochdale

A battery works with solar and without it: charged overnight on an off-peak tariff and run down through the day, it cuts the unit price of everything it covers. Our [home battery installation](/services/home-battery-installations/) page covers sizing and how the tariff side fits together.

For [EV charger installation](/services/electric-vehicle-charger-installations/), the same rules apply here as anywhere - the supply, earthing and consumer unit get checked by a qualified electrician before anything's quoted, because a charger is a new circuit on your installation, not a gadget on a wall. With the M62 on the doorstep and the tram running into Manchester via [Oldham](/oldham/), plenty of households round here rack up the miles, and overnight home charging on a cheap tariff is the affordable way to cover them.

## Electrical safety in older properties

Plenty of the housing stock across Rochdale and Heywood has been wired and rewired in stages over decades, and the state of the consumer unit and earthing affects what can be added to it. An [EICR electrical safety inspection](/services/electrical-safety-inspections-eicr/) costs £150 plus VAT for any domestic property, with same day certificates - a sensible starting point before adding solar, a battery or a charger to an older installation. We carry out remedial work and consumer unit upgrades too.
