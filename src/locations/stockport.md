## Solar panels in Stockport

Stockport's housing runs from red-brick terraces around the town centre, through the interwar semis that make up so much of the borough, to the larger detached properties of Bramhall, Cheadle Hulme and the edges towards the Peak. Each type wants something different from a [solar panel installation in Stockport](/stockport/solar-and-battery-installations/): the terraces need careful placement round chimneys, the semis have two faces to choose between, and the detached homes often have room for systems sized to the household's actual usage with a [battery](/services/home-battery-installations/) holding the evening load.

The hills and valleys through the borough mean aspect varies street to street, and the mature trees on the leafier roads put shade checks high on the survey list. Measuring what the roof really gets beats assuming, and it's the difference between a system that performs and one that merely exists.
